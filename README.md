# IV

## Problem Description

**Customer:**

A clothing agency company in South China

**Problem Description:**

Frontline operators of the clothing agency company face two major obstacles when using its internal personnel information database. These obstacles prevent them from effectively reaching and serving consumers, ultimately affecting transaction conversion.

1. **Confusion Between User Identities and Loss of Potential Customers**: The personnel information table in the database stores both consumers' and employees' information. Each data entry includes a WeChat ID field, but the two identities are not clearly distinguished. Operators can only rely on manual notes in WeChat to identify consumers, resulting in a large number of potential consumers without notes being overlooked and unable to receive advertising promotions and communications.
2. **Lack of Inefficient Promotion**: Even if some consumers are identified and followed up through notes, operators still lack key preference information about these consumers—such as their preferred clothing styles and acceptable consumption levels. This leads to a mismatch between promoted products and consumer needs; most consumers show low interest, making it difficult to complete transactions smoothly.

**Notes:**

1. The database of the clothing agency company includes a personnel information table and a consumer consumption record table. The consumer consumption record table records information such as purchased products and consumption amounts.
2. WeChat is a social media platform in China. The company’s merchants add consumers as contacts on WeChat and promote products in two ways: first, sending advertisements and communicating with consumers individually; second, sending product advertisements in customer WeChat groups organized by the merchant.
3. "WeChat notes" refer to the labels that the company’s merchants add to WeChat contacts to distinguish the attributes of different contacts.
