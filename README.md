# IV

## Problem Description

**Customer:**

The owner of a small online clothing store

**Problem Description:**

The owner of a small online clothing store has a group of highly loyal repeat customers among its core customer base. The owner insists on regularly sending product advertisements tailored to each repeat customer’s unique preferences and consumption capacity. However, as the number of repeat customers keeps rising, the owner is simply unable to remember the personalized needs of every single customer. Before sending promotional advertisements each time, the owner has to check through each customer’s past consumption records one by one, and verify the styles of products they purchased as well as their spending amounts to determine their needs. This repetitive checking and verification process usually takes an entire day. It not only eats into the time allocated for core business tasks such as stock purchasing, but also often results in delayed promotions due to the excessive time consumed.
