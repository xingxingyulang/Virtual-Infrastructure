## User Story

***

#### [HU1] Ms. Li, an online clothing retail store operator

***

Ms. Li is a 35-year-old woman who has been running an online clothing store for 3 years. Her store has accumulated a group of highly loyal repeat customers. As the number of repeat customers has grown from 50 to more than 200, she faces challenges in carrying out personalized product promotions for these old customers. She needs to customize promotional content based on each repeat customer's dressing preferences and consumption capacity. However, before sending each promotion, she has to check each customer's historical consumption records one by one, confirming the style of clothing they purchased and their spending amount to determine their needs. This repetitive checking process usually takes a whole day, which not only eats into the time allocated for core business tasks such as purchasing stock but also often results in delayed promotions due to the excessive time consumed.
